# Nimbus Cloud Storage – Plans, Limits & Billing FAQ

## Which plans are available?

Nimbus offers three plans. **Starter** is free and includes 15 GB of storage for one user. **Team** costs $8 per user per month (billed annually) or $10 month-to-month, and includes 1 TB of pooled storage per user. **Enterprise** is priced per contract and adds unlimited storage, single sign-on, audit logs and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## What is the maximum file size?

Starter accounts can upload files up to 2 GB each. Team and Enterprise accounts can upload files up to 100 GB each. Uploads larger than 5 GB are automatically split into parts and resumed if the connection drops.

## How long are deleted files kept?

Deleted files go to the Trash. On Starter they are kept for 30 days; on Team for 90 days; on Enterprise the retention period is configurable from 30 days up to 10 years. After that period files are permanently erased and cannot be recovered by support.

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Enterprise customers can bring their own encryption keys through AWS KMS or Azure Key Vault.

## Where is my data stored?

Starter and Team data is stored in the region closest to the account owner at sign-up: US, EU (Frankfurt) or Asia-Pacific (Singapore). Enterprise customers choose their region and can request data residency in a single country where Nimbus has a data centre.

## Can I get a refund?

Annual Team plans can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. After 14 days, you can cancel at any time and your plan stays active until the end of the paid period, but unused months are not refunded. Month-to-month plans can be cancelled at any time and are not refunded for the current month.

## What happens if I go over my storage limit?

You get an email warning at 80% and 95% usage. Once you reach 100%, existing files remain accessible and shareable, but new uploads and sync are paused until you free up space or upgrade.

## How do I contact support?

Starter users can use the help centre and community forum. Team users get email support with a response within one business day. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support and a named account manager.
