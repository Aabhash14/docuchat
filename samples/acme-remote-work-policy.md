# Acme Corp – Remote Work Policy

_Version 3.2 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: People Operations_

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who can work remotely at Acme Corp, how often, and what we expect from everyone working away from the office. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors follow the terms of their own agreements.

## 2. Eligibility

Employees become eligible for remote work after completing their 90-day probation period. Roles that require on-site equipment, such as warehouse operations and hardware lab technicians, are not eligible for regular remote work, but may request occasional remote days for administrative tasks with their manager's approval.

## 3. How often you can work remotely

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **three days per week**. Every team agrees on at least one shared "anchor day" each week when everyone is in the office. Tuesday is the default anchor day unless the team chooses another day and records it on the team page.

Fully remote arrangements are possible for employees who live more than 80 km from their nearest Acme office. These must be approved by the department head and reviewed every 12 months.

## 4. Working hours and availability

Remote employees keep the same working hours as office-based colleagues. Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone**. During core hours you should be reachable on chat and able to join a video call within 15 minutes.

## 5. Equipment and expenses

Acme provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset for every remote-eligible employee. Each employee also receives a one-time **home office allowance of €500** for a desk, chair or lighting, claimed through the expenses portal with receipts within 60 days of purchase.

Internet costs are reimbursed up to **€30 per month**. Co-working space memberships are not reimbursed unless the employee is on an approved fully remote arrangement, in which case up to €200 per month is covered.

## 6. Security

Work must be done on Acme-managed devices only. Always connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Do not work on confidential material in public spaces where your screen can be seen, and lock your device whenever you step away. Report lost or stolen equipment to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## 7. Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**, as long as the country is on the approved list maintained by Legal. Requests must be submitted at least three weeks in advance through the HR portal. Longer stays require a tax and immigration review and are not guaranteed.

## 8. Changes and exceptions

Managers can temporarily require more office days during onboarding, team events or critical project phases, with at least one week's notice. Exceptions to this policy are approved by People Operations. Questions can be sent to people-ops@acme.example.
